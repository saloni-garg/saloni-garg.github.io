Saloni Garg

Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Adobe Firefly Services

San Jose, California

Saloni Garg works on GPU-accelerated AI systems for large-scale generative models at Adobe, including multimodal post-training, inference optimization, and LLM evaluation. She previously spent five years at Wayfair as a Senior Software Engineer, in Berlin and the United States.

She is co-author of the textbook RAG Artificial Intelligence: Retrieval-Augmented Generation in Generative AI (De Gruyter, 2026), recipient of the 2026 CITRIS Athena Award for early career leadership, and a 2025 WLDA Awards finalist in AI for Good Impact.

Focus: LLM post-training / RLHF and RLVR / verifiers and evaluation / inference systems / retrieval-augmented generation / vector search
