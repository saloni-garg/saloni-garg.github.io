Saloni Garg

Staff Machine Learning Engineer, Adobe Firefly

San Jose, California

Saloni Garg is a Staff Machine Learning Engineer at Adobe Firefly, where she is Tech Lead for post-training multimodal LLM optimizations and technical lead for distillation of diffusion models used in video generation and editing. Her work covers post-training infrastructure, RLHF pipelines, and high-throughput GPU training.

She previously worked at Wayfair as a Senior Machine Learning Engineer and a Machine Learning Engineer, and as a Data Scientist at The Linux Foundation. She is co-author of a textbook on retrieval-augmented generation (De Gruyter, 2026) and recipient of the 2026 CITRIS Athena Award.

Focus: post-training / distillation / RLHF, SFT and DPO / GPU training infrastructure (PyTorch, CUDA, FSDP, NCCL) / retrieval-augmented generation
