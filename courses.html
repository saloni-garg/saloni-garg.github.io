02LLM Evals From Zero

A course on evaluating LLM applications: why demos mislead, how to design an evaluation suite, and how to build the golden dataset it runs on.

  • Chapter 1: Why Your App Is Already Broken (And Demos Won't Tell You)

    Why "looks good" is not a metric. A support RAG system under test, a prompt change that appears to improve quality, and why manual spot-checks pass while the system is broken.

  • Chapter 2: Anatomy of an LLM Evaluation Suite

    What an eval suite actually looks like, and how to design one: evaluation is multi-dimensional, and a single accuracy number can stay flat while quality moves.
